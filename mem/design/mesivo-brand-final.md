# Mesivo Brand Identity

## Logo: The Grid
- 4 asymmetric squares.
- One square (top-right) always in **Copper** (#D87A43).
- Wordmark: lowercase "mesivo" in **Sora** (or Manrope UI fallback).

## Palette
- **Deep Forest** (#173A34): Primary brand color, headers, primary buttons.
- **Cream** (#F8F5EF): Background, surfaces.
- **Copper** (#D87A43): Accent, primary CTA highlights, logo focal point.
- **Sage** (#5F8D67): Secondary brand accent, success states.
- **Error** (#C44747): Destructive states.
- **Text** (#232333): High-contrast typography.

## Typography
- **Sora**: Display, headings, logo wordmark.
- **Manrope**: UI, body text, functional interface.
- **IBM Plex Mono**: Numbers, data, code, operational status.
